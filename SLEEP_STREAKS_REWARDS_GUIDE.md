# Sleep Streaks & Rewards - Quick Guide

## 🔥 Sleep Streaks

### What are Sleep Streaks?
Sleep streaks track consecutive nights you meet your bedtime target (within your consistency window). The more consistent you are, the higher your streak!

### How It's Calculated
- **On Target:** Bedtime within ±30 minutes (or your custom setting) of your target
- **Streak Count:** Consecutive nights meeting target
- **Best Streak:** Your all-time record
- **Total Nights:** Cumulative nights on target

### Streak Badges
Earn badges as you build your streak:

| Badge | Name | Requirement | Points |
|-------|------|-------------|--------|
| 🌟 | First Night | 1 night on target | +50 |
| 🐦 | Early Bird | 3-night streak | +50 |
| 🔥 | Week Warrior | 7-night streak | +50 |
| ⭐ | Fortnight Champion | 14-night streak | +50 |
| 👑 | Month Master | 30-night streak | +50 |
| 💤 | Consistent Sleeper | 50 total nights | +50 |

### Motivational Messages
- **0 nights, 0 total:** "🎯 Start your streak tonight by meeting your bedtime target!"
- **0 nights, but has history:** "💪 Don't break the chain! Get back on track tonight."
- **7+ nights:** "🌟 Amazing! You're building a strong sleep routine!"

## 🎁 Rewards System

### How Points Work
Every sleep action earns points toward your overall wellness level:

| Action | Points | When |
|--------|--------|------|
| Morning Check-In | 15 | Complete daily check-in |
| Wind-Down Complete | 10 | Finish evening routine |
| On-Target Bedtime | 20 | Auto-awarded (calculated in streak) |
| Coaching Task | 25 | Complete any task |
| Week Complete | 100 | Finish all tasks in a week |
| Streak Milestone | 50 | Hit 3, 7, 14, or 30 days |
| Badge Earned | 50 | Earn any badge |

### Level System
Points accumulate across ALL modules (sleep, habits, activities, etc.):

```
Level 1:      0 points  ──────────┐
Level 2:    100 points             │
Level 3:    250 points             │ Early Levels
Level 4:    500 points             │ (Fast Progress)
Level 5:  1,000 points  ──────────┘
Level 10: 7,500 points
Level 15: 25,000 points
```

## 📱 User Experience Flow

### Daily Routine
**Evening (Before Bed):**
1. Open Sleep Hub → Click "Wind-Down"
2. Select audio track → Complete checklist
3. Click "I'm Ready for Bed" → **+10 points**
4. Bedtime logged automatically

**Morning (After Waking):**
1. Open Sleep Hub → Click "Check-In"
2. Select mood → Enter wake time
3. Optional: Reflect on what helped sleep
4. Click "Complete Check-In" → **+15 points**
5. Sleep streak updates automatically

**Dashboard Updates:**
- Streak counter increments (if on target)
- Total points increase
- May earn badge or milestone bonus
- Level may increase

### Weekly Routine (Optional)
**Coaching Program:**
1. Dashboard → "4-Week Sleep Program"
2. Enroll when prompted (commitment dialog)
3. Complete tasks throughout the week:
   - Check off each task → **+25 points each**
4. Complete all 4 tasks → **+100 bonus points**
5. Next week unlocks automatically

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Streak not updating?
- **Cause:** Dashboard hasn't been refreshed
- **Fix:** Navigate away and back to dashboard
- **Technical:** Streak calculates on dashboard load

### Points not showing?
- **Cause:** RewardsService not initialized
- **Fix:** Complete any action (check-in, wind-down)
- **Technical:** Rewards auto-initialize on first award

### Badge not appearing?
- **Cause:** Requirements met but modal not opened
- **Fix:** Click badge button (trophy icon with count)
- **Technical:** Badges awarded but must view modal

### Level stuck?
- **Cause:** Need more points for next level
- **Fix:** Check "Points to Next Level" on any rewards screen
- **Technical:** Exponential threshold system
